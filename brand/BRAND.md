# KevenOS brand

KevenOS is **the event-day operating system**. The identity should feel calm
under pressure, precise enough for operators and welcoming enough for guests.

## Logo

The K signal mark combines three ideas: a coordinated path, a live pulse and a
forward cue. Use the horizontal lockup when space permits and the mark alone for
icons. Keep clear space equal to one quarter of the mark width. Do not rotate,
recolour individual paths, add shadows or place the mark on a busy image.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Signal cyan | `#22D3EE` | live state, links, active cues |
| Motion violet | `#8B5CF6` | secondary accent and progression |
| Event night | `#07111F` | primary background and dark mark |
| Control surface | `#0B1B30` | cards, navigation and panels |
| Warm white | `#F8FAFC` | primary dark-mode text |
| Quiet blue | `#B8CBE0` | supporting text |
| Success mint | `#8AE9D9` | ready, connected and accepted states |

## Type and voice

Use Inter when available and the system sans-serif stack otherwise. Headlines
are short and direct. Product copy favors verbs: prepare, run, respond, reveal.
Avoid hype and ticketing language; KevenOS coordinates the live experience.

## Naming

Write **KevenOS**, preserving the capital K, O and S. Product areas may use
descriptive suffixes: KevenOS Desk, KevenOS Live, KevenOS Participant and
KevenOS Agent API.
