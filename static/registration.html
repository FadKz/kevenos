Event-day checklist
  1. Look up the participant. For a walk-in, confirm their name and choose an open team slot.
  2. Check them in and tell them their team, station, and round. If a teammate is missing, use the open-slot filter to find options.
  3. Show their QR privately and ask them to confirm the Participant page opens. Issue a typed password if they cannot scan.
  4. Use a fresh QR for sign-in help. Confirm identity before a password reset or team move. A move requires new access at the destination.
  5. Send any changes to teams already judging or scored to the Scoring Desk.
