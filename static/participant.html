            Before you pitch
              How points are awarded

              The preliminary checklist is worth up to 100 points before bonuses. If totals tie, the judge’s 1–5 rating for overall impression and confidence is the first tiebreaker. In the Final, each panel judge scores the pitch from 1 to 5.

                Worked practice example

                A restaurant group moving its head office

                1. Discover who uses laptops, who approves the purchase, and what has to work on moving day.
                2. Link battery life to field visits, automated setup to IT’s workload, and trade-in to the business case.
                3. Ask how their existing tools manage laptops. Explain how implementation partner can test the setup with their IT team.
                4. Ask for a Discovery meeting before the move and agree who from IT joins and when.

                  Start here · No preparation needed

                  A judge plays your customer. Your team asks about their needs, explains how solution device and implementation partner can help, and agrees a next step.

                  1. Arrive. Collect your badge and pack, find your team, then watch the training and example pitch.
                  2. Prepare together. Read your scenario and plan four speaking cues in My Pitch. Every team member must speak. Ask a coach if you get stuck.
                  3. Pitch when called. Go to your assigned station. You have 10 minutes to pitch, 3 minutes for feedback, and 2 minutes for changeover.
                  4. Review and nominate. Look for your private feedback in Results. Three finalists pitch their original scenario again in 8 minutes. Before you leave, nominate three companies as a team.

                  Prepare your pitch

                  About 2 minutes · Silent with on-screen text

                  Quick prep checklist
                  1. Read your scenario card together. Pick discovery questions and decide who speaks in each step.
                  2. Connect solution device to this customer’s people, IT and business. Choose a solution device for two roles.
                  3. Rehearse one IT objection and one business objection. Identify a relevant implementation partner service.
                  4. Ask for a free Discovery meeting. Explain why, name who should join and propose a date.
                  5. Save short cues for each step in My Pitch. Rehearse the handoffs aloud once. The deck suggests splitting 45 minutes into 12 for Discover, 12 for Solution at Work, 12 for Solve and Close, and 9 to rehearse.
